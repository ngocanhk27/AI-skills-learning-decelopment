## What belongs here

- New model, tool and protocol releases
- Trends and techniques worth watching
- Verdicts using the rings **adopt** · **trial** · **assess** · **hold** (add one as a tag)
- Promotion: when you've tried something, turn the note into a full topic in its area

!!! tip "Area tip"
    Keep radar notes short and dated. Tag each with its ring (`adopt`, `trial`, `assess`, `hold`) so the [tags page](../resources/tags.md) works as a radar view.
